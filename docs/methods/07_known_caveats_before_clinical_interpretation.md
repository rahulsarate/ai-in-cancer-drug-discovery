# Known Caveats Before Clinical Interpretation

This project is for research prioritization only.

## Key caveats

- EMT signatures are context-dependent and can differ across cancer types.
- Bulk RNA-seq can confuse malignant EMT with stromal or CAF abundance.
- CRISPR dependency often reflects proliferation, not invasion or metastasis.
- Drug-response screens usually measure viability, not anti-metastatic behavior.
- LINCS signature reversal is hypothesis-generating, not proof of therapeutic effect.
- TGF-beta, immune, and ferroptosis targets need careful safety review.
- Public datasets are biased toward primary tumors and common cancer types.

## Minimum validation before experimental prioritization

1. Confirm target expression in malignant EMT-like cells using single-cell data.
2. Check normal tissue expression and safety evidence.
3. Confirm perturbation evidence in a relevant cancer lineage.
4. Prefer orthogonal evidence from expression, dependency, drug response, and literature.
